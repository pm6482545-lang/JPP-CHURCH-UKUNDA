Welcome to JPP Ukunda

Worship, praise and the word

Our Ukunda church gathers to worship God, hear the word taught plainly, and walk through life together. Everyone is welcome, from first-time visitors to families who have grown up in church.

Getting to us

Easy to reach from Diani

We serve Ukunda and the surrounding Diani area. Call or WhatsApp 0743 264 326 and we will gladly share directions and the current service times.

Our vision

A transformed church, transforming others

We are a transformed church of God through Christ, called to transform others — carrying the change we've received in Him into our homes, our streets, and our nation.

Our mission

Right knowledge, right image of God

To impact generations with the knowledge from the word of God, making everyone have the right image of God and His will for their lives.
